# NEXORA SP'26 — CSS Flagship Two-Day Event

> NEXORA SP'26 was the Computer Science Society's flagship two-day event at COMSATS Sahiwal, bringing together students through technical competitions, gaming tournaments, creative activities, and campus-wide experiences. It recorded 300+ attendees and was described as the highest-footfall event ever held at COMSATS Sahiwal.

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Date** | 19–20 May 2026 |
| **Category** | University Event / Technical & Recreational Festival |
| **Organization** | Computer Science Society (CSS), COMSATS University Islamabad, Sahiwal Campus |
| **My Role** | Founding President / Event Founder & Lead |
| **Location** | COMSATS University Islamabad, Sahiwal Campus |
| **Duration** | 2 Days |
| **Footfall** | 300+ attendees |

## Event Overview

NEXORA SP'26 was developed as a flagship CSS initiative to bring together technical learning, competition, creativity, entertainment, and student engagement through a two-day campus event.

The event featured a diverse lineup of technical competitions, gaming tournaments, recreational activities, and social experiences, creating opportunities for students from different disciplines and semesters to participate.

## My Contributions

- Founded and led NEXORA as the inaugural flagship event under my presidency of the Computer Science Society.
- Developed and presented the overall event concept and proposed two-day schedule.
- Chaired the planning meeting on 13 May 2026 alongside the General Secretary, Muhammad Ahmad Nawaz.
- Led planning discussions covering competition categories, rules, judging criteria, sponsorship, budget, and resource requirements.
- Established organizing committees for registration, logistics, media coverage, judging coordination, and on-ground volunteer management.
- Coordinated the event's promotional strategy, including social media campaigns, posters, class announcements, and registration planning.
- Oversaw the coordination of technical and recreational activities across both event days.
- Led the society's efforts to deliver an event that brought together 300+ attendees.
