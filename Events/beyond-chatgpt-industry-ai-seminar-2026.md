## Seminar Overview

The seminar featured Hassan Raza, a COMSATS Sahiwal alumnus and Senior Software Engineer at Search Atlas.

Titled *Beyond ChatGPT: How Industry Is Actually Using AI*, the session explored how Artificial Intelligence is being applied across the technology industry beyond the popular use of conversational AI tools.

Through industry insights and practical perspectives, students gained a better understanding of real-world AI applications and the evolving role of AI in technology.

## Key Discussion Areas

- Practical applications of AI across the industry.
- AI beyond conversational tools such as ChatGPT.
- How organizations are adopting AI in real-world environments.
- Industry perspectives on the evolving AI landscape.
