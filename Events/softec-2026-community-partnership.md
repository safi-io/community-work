# SOFTEC'26 — Community Partnership & Student Delegation

> Represented the Computer Science Society (CSS), COMSATS Sahiwal, and Sahiwal Tech Community at SOFTEC'26, leading a delegation of 40 students to one of Pakistan's major university technology events.

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Date** | 17–19 April 2026 |
| **Category** | Technology Festival / Community Partnership |
| **Organizations** | Computer Science Society (CSS), COMSATS Sahiwal; Sahiwal Tech Community |
| **My Role** | Student Delegation Lead / Community Ambassador |
| **Partner Event** | SOFTEC'26 |
| **Venue** | FAST NUCES, Lahore, Pakistan |
| **Delegation** | 40 students |

## Contributions

- Led a delegation of 40 students from COMSATS Sahiwal to attend SOFTEC'26 in Lahore.
- Represented CSS and Sahiwal Tech Community as a community ambassador.
- Coordinated student participation and facilitated the delegation's involvement in the event.
- Helped connect students from Sahiwal with a broader technology, innovation, and university community.
- Contributed to strengthening inter-university engagement and technology community connections.

## Community Partnership

The Computer Science Society (CSS), COMSATS Sahiwal, was officially recognized as a **Community Partner for SOFTEC'26**.

This partnership provided an opportunity to connect the Sahiwal student community with SOFTEC's technology-focused activities and broader network.
