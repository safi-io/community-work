## Contributions

- Contributed to the session through my recent experience of passing the AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner (CLF-C02) examination.
- Shared practical insights into the AWS certification journey and preparation experience.
- Engaged with students by answering their questions about AWS certifications and exam preparation.
- Represented both AWS Student Builder Group — CUI Sahiwal and the Computer Science Society in their collaborative effort to support students pursuing cloud certifications.

## Session Highlights

The session covered:

- AWS certification exam structure and exam blueprints.
- Effective preparation strategies.
- Career impact and opportunities associated with AWS certifications.
- Student questions and practical guidance.

Up to 20 attendees had the opportunity to receive fully sponsored AWS certification exam vouchers.
