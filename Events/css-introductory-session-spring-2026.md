# CSS Introductory Session — Spring 2026

> The official opening session of the Computer Science Society's Spring 2026 chapter, introducing students to the society's vision, activities, and opportunities for involvement.

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Date** | 18 February 2026 |
| **Category** | Society Orientation / Introductory Session |
| **Organization** | Computer Science Society (CSS), COMSATS University Islamabad, Sahiwal Campus |
| **My Role** | Presenter / Society President |
| **Location** | C4, C Block, COMSATS Sahiwal |
| **Audience** | Nearly 100 students |
| **Duration** | 12:45 PM – 01:40 PM |

## Contributions

- Presented the introductory session as President of the Computer Science Society.
- Introduced CSS's motto, vision, and role within the university's Computer Science community.
- Shared an overview of upcoming events, activities, and opportunities for students to participate.
- Welcomed new students and encouraged them to become actively involved in the society.
- Contributed to launching the Spring 2026 chapter and strengthening student engagement.

## Event Highlights

- Nearly 100 students attended the session.
- Swag items were distributed among attendees.
- The session marked the official beginning of CSS's Spring 2026 chapter.
