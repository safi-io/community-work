# How to Navigate Through a CS Degree for Maximum Excellence

> A guidance workshop for first-semester Computer Science students, covering academic planning, university life, programming, projects, and effective use of generative AI tools.

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Date** | 13 November 2025 |
| **Category** | Technical Workshop / Student Mentorship |
| **Organization** | Computer Science Society (CSS), COMSATS University Islamabad, Sahiwal Campus |
| **My Role** | Workshop Instructor / Mentor |
| **Location** | COMSATS University Islamabad, Sahiwal Campus |
| **Audience** | Approximately 60–70 students, primarily first-semester students |
| **Duration** | 1 hour |

## Contributions

- Delivered a workshop focused on helping first-semester students navigate their Computer Science degree effectively.
- Guided students on choosing programming languages and developing a productive university routine.
- Discussed practical ways to use generative AI tools effectively.
- Addressed questions about semester projects, academic expectations, and university life.
- Encouraged peer mentorship and the continuation of a culture of guidance within the student community.

## Event Highlights

- Approximately 60–70 students attended.
- The audience had an almost equal gender ratio.
- The session featured an interactive discussion, with students raising questions about academic and practical challenges.
