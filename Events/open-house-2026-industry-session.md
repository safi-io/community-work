# Open House 2026 — Industry Insights Session

> An industry-focused session connecting COMSATS Sahiwal students with real-world insights into how technology companies operate, the skills they expect, and the professional workplace culture.

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Date** | 23 April 2026 |
| **Category** | Industry Seminar / Career Development |
| **Organization** | Computer Science Society (CSS), COMSATS University Islamabad, Sahiwal Campus |
| **My Role** | Event Lead / End-to-End Organizer |
| **Speaker** | Arslan Maqsood, Staff Full Stack Developer at Andela |
| **Location** | Auditorium, A Block, COMSATS Sahiwal |
| **Audience** | 150+ students |
| **Duration** | 12:00 PM – 01:30 PM |

## Contributions

- Led the event end to end, taking responsibility for planning, coordination, and execution.
- Reached out to and coordinated with the industry speaker, Arslan Maqsood, to bring professional insights to the campus.
- Managed the arrangements required to organize the session, from initial communication through event-day execution.
- Coordinated with the society and relevant university stakeholders to ensure smooth delivery.
- Helped create an opportunity for students to engage directly with an experienced software engineering professional.

## Event Highlights

- Welcomed more than 150 students.
- Explored how technology companies operate and what they expect from aspiring professionals.
- Covered industry expectations, essential technical and professional skills, and workplace culture.
- Included career guidance and an interactive discussion addressing students' questions and concerns.
