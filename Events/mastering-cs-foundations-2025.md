
# Mastering CS Foundations: A Guided Approach

> A technical workshop and mentorship session focused on strengthening core computer science fundamentals, helping students navigate their CS degrees and build a strong foundation for their careers.

## Contributions

- Led a session on essential computer science foundations, covering Programming Fundamentals (PF), Object-Oriented Programming (OOP), Data Structures and Algorithms (DSA), and Databases.
- Guided junior students on navigating their computer science degrees and developing strong technical fundamentals.
- Addressed students' questions about programming languages, learning paths, and academic decisions.
- Shared practical academic guidance and encouraged peer-to-peer mentorship.
