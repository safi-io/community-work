# CSS Career Week 2026 — Explore. Learn. Decide.

> A five-day career exploration initiative launched for the first time by CSS, bringing industry professionals and students together through five focused seminars across major technology and professional domains.

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Date** | 19–23 October 2026 |
| **Category** | Career Development Initiative / Industry Seminar Series |
| **Organization** | Computer Science Society (CSS), COMSATS University Islamabad, Sahiwal Campus |
| **My Role** | Initiative Founder / President / Event Lead / DE Seminar Speaker |
| **Duration** | 5 Days |
| **Sessions** | 5 Seminars across 5 Career Domains |

## Event Overview

CSS Career Week 2026 was initiated for the first time to help students explore career opportunities, understand industry expectations, and make informed decisions about their professional futures.

Under the theme **"Explore. Learn. Decide."**, the five-day initiative featured five seminars led by industry professionals, each dedicated to a different domain within the technology and professional landscape.

### Five Career Domains

1. **Web Development**
2. **Mobile Development**
3. **Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (AI/ML)**
4. **Data Engineering**
5. **Freelancing**

## Contributions

- Initiated and introduced the first CSS Career Week at COMSATS Sahiwal.
- Developed the concept of a five-day career exploration initiative built around five technology and professional domains.
- Led the overall planning and coordination of the initiative as CSS President.
- Structured the program around five seminars featuring industry professionals.
- Coordinated the agenda and session planning across the five domains.
- Created awareness among students about diverse career paths and opportunities in technology.
- Personally created a video explaining the motivation behind Career Week and encouraging students to participate.
