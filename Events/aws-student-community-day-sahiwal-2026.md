# AWS Student Community Day Sahiwal 2026

> A student-focused AWS community event bringing together developers, learners, and technology enthusiasts for technical talks, hands-on activities, networking, and community engagement.

## Event Overview

AWS Student Community Day Sahiwal 2026 brought the local student and technology community together for a day of learning, building, coding, and networking. The event featured technical talks, activities, opportunities to connect with fellow developers, and AWS community engagement.

The event was organized through collaboration between AWS Student Builder Group at COMSATS Sahiwal, CSS, and Sahiwal Tech Community, with CSS serving as a Community Partner.

## Contributions

- Served as a core organizer, contributing to the planning and execution of the event.
- Represented AWS Student Builder Group as Executive Coordinator and CSS as President.
- Contributed to community partnership and coordination across the participating organizations.
- Supported on-ground operations and event management.
- Helped coordinate activities and operational requirements to deliver the event experience.
- Contributed to bringing together students and members of Sahiwal's technology community.
