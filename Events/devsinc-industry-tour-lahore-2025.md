# Devsinc Industry Tour — Lahore

> Organized an industry tour to Devsinc Lahore, providing COMSATS Sahiwal students and faculty with practical exposure to the software industry and a professional workplace.

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| **Date** | 5 December 2025 |
| **Category** | Industry Tour / Industry-Academia Engagement |
| **Organization** | Department of Computer Science, COMSATS University Islamabad, Sahiwal Campus; Devsinc |
| **My Role** | Organizer / Devsinc Campus Ambassador |
| **Location** | Devsinc Software House, Plot B-281, Ghazi Road, Lahore, Pakistan |

| **Duration** | Full-day visit |
| **Distance** | More than 400 km round trip |

## Contributions

- Initiated and organized an industry tour to Devsinc Lahore in collaboration with the Department of Computer Science, COMSATS Sahiwal.
- Led the initiative as a Devsinc Campus Ambassador, connecting the university community with the software industry.
- Coordinated approximately four months of planning, communication, and logistics.
- Managed transportation arrangements, fuel, food, participant payments, and the overall schedule.
- Led the journey from Sahiwal to Lahore and back, coordinating the group throughout the day.
- Helped ensure students were safely dropped off after returning to Sahiwal.
