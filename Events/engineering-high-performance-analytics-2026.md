## Workshop Overview

The workshop focused on the engineering concepts behind modern analytical systems and the techniques used to process large volumes of data efficiently.

It introduced students to columnar storage, compression techniques, and modern OLAP engines, explaining how these technologies contribute to faster analytical queries and efficient data processing.

## Key Discussion Areas

- **Columnar Storage:** Understanding column-oriented data organization and its advantages for analytical workloads.
- **Data Compression:** Exploring compression techniques and their role in reducing storage requirements and improving query performance.
- **Modern OLAP Engines:** Understanding the fundamentals of analytical database engines and their architecture.
- **High-Performance Analytics:** Examining how storage formats, compression, and query execution contribute to efficient analytical systems.
