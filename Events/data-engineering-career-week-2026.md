## Session Overview

As part of CSS Career Week 2026, I delivered a session introducing students to the field of Data Engineering.

The session explored how data is collected, processed, and managed to power modern technologies, while providing industry insights to help students understand the field and consider it as a career path.

## Contributions

- Delivered an introductory session on Data Engineering as part of the five-day CSS Career Week initiative.
- Explained the role of data collection, processing, and management in modern technology systems.
- Shared industry insights from my experience as a Data Platform Engineer at KashEdge AI.
- Helped students understand the Data Engineering career path and its relevance in the technology industry.
